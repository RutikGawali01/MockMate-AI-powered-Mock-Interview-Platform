# 🎤 MockMate - AI powered Mock Interview Platform

An AI-powered interview preparation platform that helps candidates practice Technical and HR interviews through personalized question generation, voice-based interactions, intelligent feedback, and detailed performance reports.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📄 Resume-Based Interview Generation

* Upload your resume to generate personalized interview questions.
* Questions are tailored based on:

  * Resume content
  * Target job role
  * Experience level
* Supports both Technical and HR interview rounds.

### 🤖 AI Interview Simulation

* AI interviewer asks questions using natural voice interaction.
* Male and female AI interviewer options.
* Realistic interview experience with timed question rounds.

### 🎙️ Voice & Text Responses

* Answer questions using:

  * Voice (Speech-to-Text)
  * Text Input
* Seamless switching between voice and text responses.

### ⏱️ Timed Interview Sessions

* Configurable time limit for each question.
* Automatic answer submission when time expires.
* Progress tracking throughout the interview.

### 📊 Intelligent Feedback System

* AI evaluates each response in real time.
* Provides:

  * Strengths
  * Weaknesses
  * Improvement Suggestions
  * Performance Insights

### 📈 Performance Analytics

* Overall interview score.
* Detailed answer analysis.
* Historical interview records.
* Track improvement over multiple interview sessions.

### 📄 PDF Report Generation

* Download comprehensive interview reports.
* Includes:

  * Question-wise feedback
  * Performance summary
  * Improvement recommendations

### 💳 Credit-Based Access System

* Secure credit purchasing system.
* Credits used to unlock interview sessions.
* Integrated payment gateway for seamless transactions.

### 🔐 Authentication & Security

* Google Authentication.
* Secure user sessions.
* Protected interview and payment workflows.

## 📋 Interview Workflow

1. Sign in using Google Authentication.
2. Upload your resume.
3. Select target role and experience level.
4. AI generates personalized interview questions.
5. Attend Technical or HR interview rounds.
6. Answer using voice or text.
7. Receive real-time AI feedback.
8. Download detailed PDF performance reports.
9. Track interview history and improvement over time.
